Privacy notice

Information this website collects

You can browse and request a service without an account. If you create a customer account, this website stores your email, name, phone number, and a password hash. If you register as a provider, it stores your email, password hash, business contact details, service category, area, description, and skills; your public listing shows the business name, category, area, description, and skills.

If you send a service request, this website stores your name, phone number, selected provider, and the details you enter. When you are signed in as a customer, the request is linked to your account so you can check its status.

If you use the provider matcher, the description is sent to this website to compare it with public listings. The matcher does not save that description or send it to an outside AI service. Do not include contact details or other sensitive information in the description.

To reduce automated abuse, the website stores a keyed, pseudonymous value derived from your connection IP address with short-lived rate-limit counters. The raw IP address is not stored in those counters, and counter data is deleted within 24 hours.

How the information is used

The website owner and the provider you selected can view a request and its contact details to follow up. Other customers cannot view your name, phone number, or request details. The owner can manage the request; providers can view requests for their own listings. This pilot does not send requests automatically by email or text.

Storage and deletion

Service requests are automatically removed from the database after {{retention_days}} days. Account details are kept while the account exists. Contact the owner if you want an account or request removed sooner.
